Netlify and Google Search Guide for Vrokal

1. Open the site's Netlify URL (usually ending in netlify.app) to view the deployed site.
2. Publishing on Netlify does not make the site appear in Google immediately.
3. To ask Google to index the site, add it to Google Search Console, verify ownership, and request indexing. It can take time, and indexing is not guaranteed.
4. Vrokal was built as an Anvil app. If Anvil project files were uploaded directly to Netlify, Anvil-specific features may not work there. Open the Netlify URL to check what it shows.
